Settings

Imports

External preparation renames files and may convert them and archive originals. Leave it off to read external files in place.

New videos in the default Videos and Adverts folders follow the numbered MP4 filename format. Existing catalogue entries stay as they are. Converted originals are kept in a .usbtv-originals folder beside your videos. Changes to this switch apply on the next scan.

Audio

Applies one fixed volume adjustment per recording to reduce jumps between programmes and adverts. It preserves quiet and loud moments within a recording; existing background noise may become more noticeable when boosted. Switch off to compare the original level. Your video files stay unchanged.

Genres

Genres label your video. Choose its broadcast destinations separately under Allowed channels. Save labels also includes any genre still typed above.

Allowed channels

Programmes need at least one channel. Adverts with no selection can play on every channel.

Episode review

Videos over 50 minutes are flagged for a quick check. Films and long single programmes can be marked Single programme. Multiple episodes use markers in the original file and inherit its labels and channels.

Equal divisions are only a starting guess. Adjust each start/end (minutes:seconds or hours:minutes:seconds), preview the boundaries, then Save labels. Gaps are skipped; episodes play in order within the channel lineup. Existing broadcasts adopt changes at their next lineup update.

Internal advert break points

Use timestamps separated by commas, e.g. 15:00, 30:00. Suggestions need your approval.

Reusable labelling rules

One rule per line: keyword => genres | channels. Example: kurzgesagt => Science, Animation | Explore. Rules match whole words or phrases in filenames, folders, titles and tags.

Assign to selected rows

Comma separated. Add, replace or remove labels on selected rows; blank fields stay unchanged. Type and Broadcast apply independently of label mode. Preview changes first, then Save selected files. Selections outside the current filters are included when saving.
